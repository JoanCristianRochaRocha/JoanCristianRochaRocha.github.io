Bio

Cristian Rocha is a second-year MA student at the Universiy of Geneva. He is majoring in Technologies of Translation and Communication with a concentration in Localisation.

During his BA in Multilingual Communication, he became interested in digital communication while studying at the University Computer Centre (CUI). He learned how to code, develop digital content strategies and create software. Once his interest in this domain was sparked, Cristian decided to specialize in content marketing and project management, and aspires to pursue a career in international brand strategy and manage the visual identity of an organization.

He put his skills to practice during the 2026 ‘Traducthon’, a student-led localisation project at the University of Geneva, where he managed a team of 10 people.

Outside of his studies, Cristian is a volunteer firefighter and enjoys going climbing and travelling to practice scuba diving.

Professional career

Cristian Rocha has spent five years in front-desk, counter and administrative roles in Geneva, all held alongside his university studies.

He began at McDonald’s Plainpalais in 2018 as a crew member and cashier, and spent a month at the Grand Théâtre de Genève managing stock and filing documents. From 2021 to 2022 he worked as a counter agent at Money & Com in Gare Cornavin, handling 200 to 300 international money transfers per shift across two separate systems, to more than 25 countries, with a till reaching CHF 50,000 a day.

He then moved into hospitality. As a night receptionist at City Hostel Geneva from 2022 to 2023, he ran the front desk alone, with no supervisor on site, managing check-ins, bookings on the Opera PMS and the end-of-service till. In 2024 he joined the room service team at the Fairmont Grand Hotel Geneva, serving an international and VIP clientele in French, English and Spanish. He finished this chapter at R Pressing Retouche, where until July 2026 he handled client follow-up alongside the full administration of the business, from invoicing and tax paperwork to supplier orders.

Outside paid work, Cristian served as secretary of AIESEC and later Think Out, was a member of ESN Geneva, and has been a volunteer firefighter with Compagnie 25 in Collonge-Bellerive since 2025.

Next projects

Cristian is currently completing his MA thesis on machine translation quality in the localisation of independent visual novels, with graduation expected in June 2027. Alongside it, he is looking for an internship or an entry-level position in digital communication, with the aim of putting into practice what has so far remained largely academic. He is also continuing his training as a volunteer firefighter, working towards the final examination that will allow him to serve independently.
